Irrigation

Irrigation in agriculture is the practice of applying controlled amounts of water to land to help grow crops, landscape plants, and lawns.

Irrigation workflow

  1. Connect to water source
  2. Distribute via pipes
  3. Disperse with sprinklers
  4. Control with timers and sensors

Sensors Used

  • Soil Moisture Sensor
  • Temperature Sensor
  • Humidity Sensor
  • Rainfall Sensor
